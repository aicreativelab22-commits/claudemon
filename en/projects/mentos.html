MENTOS

In the rhythm of the city.

Purpose and idea
We added an unexpected Mentos moment to Istanbul’s everyday flow. By bringing street and seaside footage together with AI-generated scenes, we carried the idea of freshness to the scale of the city. Made with Afternoon Film Pro, the film brings real footage and imagination into one story.
What MON handled
The camera recorded, imagination kept going. Working with our shooting partner, we started with reality and opened up its limits with AI.
Outputs and formats
34-second ad film. Format: vertical, 9:16.
Status
Co-produced with Afternoon Film Pro. Made for Mentos.
