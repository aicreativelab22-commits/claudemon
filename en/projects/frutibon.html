FRUTIBON

Dark chocolate, whole fruit.

Purpose and idea
We told the story of strawberry and dark chocolate through a small moment of pleasure shared among friends. Combining product close-ups with a warm get-together scene, we brought out the feeling of sharing as much as the flavor.
What MON handled
Character and scene design, AI-assisted visual and motion production, editing and sound.
Outputs and formats
15-second film. Format: vertical, 9:16.
Status
Independent concept work. It does not claim any collaboration or client relationship with Kahve Dünyası / Frutibon.
