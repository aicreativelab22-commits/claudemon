HAPPY TO WEAR

Your day changes, your sparkle stays.

Purpose and idea
We explored a single outfit through the day’s changing light and atmosphere. Taking the beige trench coat and gold sequin skirt into a city story from morning to evening, we highlighted the collection’s character across different moments.
What MON handled
Scene design from the brand’s products, AI-assisted visual and motion production, film editing.
Outputs and formats
15-second fashion film. Format: vertical, 9:16.
Status
Made as part of a brand collaboration with Happy to Wear.
