HAPPY TO WEAR

Real shoot, limitless scenes.

Purpose and idea
Happy to Wear did the shoot and sent us the images. Starting from them, we used AI to take the same outfit into new scenes: close-ups against a black backdrop, movement in a Haussmann salon with a gold mirror. The real shoot laid the foundation; AI extended the stage.
What MON handled
AI-assisted scene and motion production from the brand’s shoot images, film editing.
Outputs and formats
Two fashion films: 12 and 13 seconds. Format: vertical, 9:16.
Status
Made as part of a brand collaboration with Happy to Wear. Photography: Happy to Wear.
