Concept brand

DAL

From root to table.

Purpose and idea
For DAL, we built a visual story that runs from the olive grove to the table. We brought warm sunlight, natural textures and metal packaging into one world. Through a short film and a nine-frame visual series, we explored the bond between where the product comes from and the moment it is enjoyed.
What MON handled
Scene design, AI-assisted visual and motion production, editing.
Outputs and formats
16-second film, nine-frame campaign visual series. Format: vertical film, 4:5 visual series.
Status
Concept brand work. Not created as part of a commercial brand collaboration.
