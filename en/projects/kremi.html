Independent concept

KREMİ

Four shades, one touch.

Purpose and idea
For four colors, we built a narrative that makes their inspirations visible. We brought the Peach, Cherry Coke, Strawberry Velvet and Coffee shades together in a short film that moves between product, flavor association, texture and the look on the lips.
What MON handled
Product and texture shots, color storytelling, AI-assisted visual and motion production, editing.
Outputs and formats
20-second film. Format: vertical, 9:16.
Status
Independent concept work. It does not claim any collaboration or client relationship with Kremi.
